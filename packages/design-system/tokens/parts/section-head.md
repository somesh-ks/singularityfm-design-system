# Section head (`section-head`)

A section header with more room above than below, so it belongs to what follows.

## Use when

- title a group of rows or cards on a page

## Where it is drawn

- Classes: `.section-head` (a page draws the component, never types these)
- Styled in: `styles/primitives.css`

## Owner's note

More room above a header than below it: the header belongs to what follows.
