A filled Button with `data-busy="true"` keeps its fill and shows a progress cursor while it is also aria-disabled, and a SearchField whose input is disabled (a disabled fieldset included) dims like every other disabled control. What looks different: a busy filled button that was grey is now in its primary fill, and a disabled search field is now dimmed. A page can drop its own copies of these two rules.
