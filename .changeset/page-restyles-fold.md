The reader's page restyles move into the library (DS-11). New experimental options, each listed by `ds find` with its reason and date and free to change or go in a minor release: `--icon-size` on the control that holds an Icon (sets the glyph's size without a rule on the glyph), IconButton `variant="filled"`, SearchField `clearable` (a Clear text button, `.search-field-wrap` and `.search-field-clear`; call `installSearchClear()` from `design-system/searchField` on each page load, and `syncSearchClear(input)` after setting the text from a script; a form reset and a page shown from the back-forward cache resync on their own), Field `tonal` (an outlined Select takes it as `data-tonal`), PlayChip `primary` and Menu `flush` (`menuListClass(sheet, flush)`). New stable states: a standard IconButton with `aria-pressed="true"` takes the accent container (a tonal or filled one keeps its own fill) and, holding two Icons, shows the second while on; a readonly Field draws itself sunken and keeps the accent edge while focused; a failed tonal select keeps its danger edge under the pointer and in focus; a `chevronDown` or `chevronUp` in an icon button or an `aria-haspopup` control turns while `aria-expanded="true"`, and in an `aria-haspopup="listbox"` trigger it takes the muted ink; the `chevronUp` glyph joins the player's registry. A page can delete its own copies of these rules. What looks different: every Play triangle (hero cover, dock, transport, queue Start listening) now sits 1/16rem right of its box, as the play chip's and the FAB's already did; a SearchField inside a `.card-group` is white in light and one grey up in dark, with the card's chip hover, instead of the container grey, and takes the focus step while focused (a page's own card gets this by being a `.card-group`); a Banner's words start at its start even inside a centred column; in a Settings or Manage row only the trailing chevron is muted and pulled out to the row's end, so another glyph there keeps its own colour and size; a tonal IconButton on a phone's menu sheet takes the sheet's tile tone.
