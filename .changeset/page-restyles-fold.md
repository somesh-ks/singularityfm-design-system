The reader's page restyles move into the library (DS-11). New experimental options, each listed by `ds find` with its reason and date and free to change or go in a minor release: `--icon-size` on the control that holds an Icon (sets the glyph's size without a rule on the glyph), IconButton `variant="filled"`, SearchField `clearable` (a Clear text button; call `installSearchClear()` from `design-system/searchField` on each page load, and `syncSearchClear(input)` after setting the text from a script), Field `tonal` (an outlined Select takes it as `data-tonal`), PlayChip `primary` and Menu `flush` (`menuListClass(sheet, flush)`). New stable states: a standard IconButton with `aria-pressed="true"` takes the accent container and, holding two Icons, shows the second while on; a readonly Field draws itself sunken; a chevron in an icon button or an `aria-haspopup` control turns while `aria-expanded="true"`; the `chevronUp` glyph joins the player's registry. A page can delete its own copies of these rules. What looks different: every Play triangle (hero cover, dock, transport, queue Start listening) now sits 1/16rem right of its box, as the play chip's and the FAB's already did; a SearchField on a card (`.card-group`, the topic card) is white in light and one grey up in dark, with the card's chip hover, instead of the container grey; a Banner's words start at its start even inside a centred column; in a Settings or Manage row only the trailing chevron is muted and pulled out to the row's end, so another glyph there keeps its own colour and size; a tonal IconButton on a phone's menu sheet takes the sheet's tile tone.
