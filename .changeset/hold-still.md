A control's box no longer depends on its state (DS-9). PlayChip takes `reserve` (the widest words it can say; "Play again" by default) and is as wide as those words in every state, cutting longer words with an ellipsis; Button, Fab and a tonal Select take `reserve` too (a script that swaps a Button's words writes `[data-button-label]`, and a Fab's words go in one child span, which a script may write); a `.spinner` child of a filled or tonal Button now shows only while `aria-busy="true"` or `data-busy="true"` is set (it was always drawn), and stands in the start padding so the words stay put; check any Button of yours that draws a spinner. A story's Play chip is about 10px wider (it fits "Play again"). A chip drawn without `data-reserve` keeps its old sizing. The words of a waiting PlayChip (`data-state="preparing"`) and of a Fab with `data-state="preparing"` now shimmer: a light band crosses the letters, which keep their colour at 70% and show full strength in the band (a mask; the `sheen` keyframes are in foundations.css), still under reduced motion and forced colours. A hero chip's `reserve` should name the widest of its words, "Processing". The shipped rules gain `raw-px` (`ds validate`, `rawPixels`, `PX_EXCEPTIONS`): a pixel length in a stylesheet is an error, except 0, the 1px hairline and a named exception (DS-10).
