Three 0.10.0 rules now meet the reader's house rules. A SearchField's forced-colours edge is `ButtonBorder`, as the picker's search is, in place of `FieldText`, so its border colour can change in a forced-colours theme. A readonly Field's hover rules sit behind the fine-pointer gate. A SearchField on a `.card-group` takes the overlay tone with no `:focus` in its selector: the focus step comes after it and wins.
