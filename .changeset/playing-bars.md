PlayingBars is a component: import it as `@singularityfm/design-system/PlayingBars` to draw the three moving bars that show something playing (the Play chip's mark), on any play control, with `className` to place it, instead of typing `chip-bars`. The Play chip draws it; its markup is the same but for `aria-hidden="true"` on the bars, inside a glyph that was already hidden.
