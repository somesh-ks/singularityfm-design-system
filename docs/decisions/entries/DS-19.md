## DS-19: Eight criteria of our own sit under the public rubric's 31

- Status: active; amends DS-14 (the rubric as the only score)
- Date: 2026-11-14
- Decision: the owner chose on a card (2026-11-14) to keep the public rubric and add our own rows, in one table on `docs/agent-readiness.md`. The 31 public criteria stay as written and alone give the floor, the retrieval level, the median and the tier. Under them, S1 to S8 score his taste rules (buttons hug their words, no pixel lengths, the 8pt grid, every mood and dark, motion from tokens, no layout shift, colour as roles) and the agent test's outcome, on the same 0 to 4 scale and strict rule, with their own line (`Ours: lowest N, median N.`) that `docsCheck` holds to the rows. Two public levels we decline are named, still scored as the rubric says: 5.2's MCP (DS-14) and 6.1's component source registry.
- Why: the public rubric cannot see the rules the owner checks on every screen or whether agents build the right thing, and a score that misses them rewards the wrong work. Rewriting the 31 in our words would leave a score only we grade, against nothing outside: why DS-14 turned down a home-made rubric.
- Rejected: only our own rubric (no outside comparison, no upgrades from the rubric's authors); our rows inside the tier (the tier would stop meaning what it means for other systems).
- Revisit if: the rubric's next version covers taste or outcomes, or a row of ours sits at 4 for three rescores and stops telling us anything.
