### Roadmap

- Quick wins: DD-002, DD-003, DD-005, DD-006 (done); DD-007 waits on the owner's call, a one-line change either way.
- Structural projects: none.
- Write-offs: DD-001, because the forced-colors paths exist (primitives.css, switch.css, aside.css and seven more).
- Backlog: DD-004.

Routed: accessibility conformance to sk-design-systems:accessibility; the track art's 500ms hover to sk-motion-design:tune-motion-tokens. Supplementary reference: `npx ui-skills get ibelick/fixing-motion-performance` (no `motion` slug exists); the accordion meets its critical and high rules (transform and opacity, one measurement per press with every read before the first write, no per-frame layout reads), and its clip-path is paint on a small, isolated surface, which it allows.

Verified in Chrome: 1024 and 1440 in both themes, the bottom bar at 360 and 390, forced colours, the accessible names on all 32 component pages, frame pacing at 4x CPU throttle, and the accordion stepped frame by frame (every animation paused and advanced 1/60 s): 34 runs of one and two presses, 17 to 200ms apart, with no position jump, a speed change at a press equal to that press's own push, the gap from each group's visible box to the row under it unchanged (0px drift), a close while the column is scrolled (120px, 200px and to the end, at 1440x900 and 1024x768, alone and interrupted) settling within 0.6px where the scroll clamps (the review had measured a jump of up to 1434px), no layout mid-flight, and a clean settle where layout puts it. Not verified: iOS Select zoom on a device; screen-reader announcement of the Toast's role order; additive clip-path composition in Safari and Firefox.
